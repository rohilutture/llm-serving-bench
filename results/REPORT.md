# Serving benchmark

Baseline: `hf-bf16`.

- Pareto-optimal on (accuracy, throughput): `vllm-bf16`, `vllm-bf16-kvfp8`, `vllm-bf16-lora`, `vllm-bf16-ngram5`, `vllm-w4a16-gptq`
- Pareto-optimal AND within 2 accuracy points of the baseline (the usable set): `vllm-bf16`, `vllm-bf16-lora`, `vllm-bf16-ngram5`, `vllm-w4a16-gptq`
- ⚠ Accuracy regression (more than 2 pts below baseline; fast but not usable): `hf-nf4`, `vllm-bf16-kvfp8`, `vllm-w4a16-awq`
